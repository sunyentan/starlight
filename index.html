Starlight - 3D Cellular Automata

Project by: Sun-Yen Tan

Inspired by: Stellar Evolution and Theory of Computation

Current Parameters:

  • Max Cells: 10000
  • Step Size: 0.5
  • Cube Size: 20
  • Sim Speed: 1.0x

Quick Tips:

  • Use the right panel to adjust simulation parameters.
  • Pause to explore individual generations.
  • Zoom in to activate microscopic view mode.
  • Click "Next" and "Prev" to step through generations manually.
